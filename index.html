What happens in a reading?

Book a time and jot down what’s on your mind. During the session, Emma shares what comes through and you can ask questions as you go. You leave with clarity, and a next step if you want one.

Can we talk by phone, or does it have to be video?

Either works. You can join by phone or by video call from anywhere, and you don’t need any special app. Email readings arrive by email within 1 business day.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Rescheduling is free with 24 hours’ notice or more. Later changes may carry a fee; the cancellation policy has the details.

Which session should I start with?

With one focused question, an Email or Quickie Reading is a gentle start. For more time and depth, try a Classic Reading. Still unsure? Draw a card.
