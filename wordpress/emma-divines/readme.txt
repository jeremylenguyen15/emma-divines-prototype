=== Emma Divines ===
Emma's homepage, editable from the dashboard.

Where to change things (Dashboard → Emma Divines):
- Site content: every heading and sentence on the homepage, your photos, phone, booking link and social links. Each tab is one part of the page.
- Sessions & events: one entry per tarot card. Name, length, price, description, Calendly link, card art and category. "Order" sets the order inside its category.
- Categories: the tabs of cards (Psychic Readings, Mediumship, Reiki, Classes) and Events. Edit each one's intro and photo, or its order.
- Reviews and FAQs: add, edit or reorder ("Order" field, lower first).
- Messages: every note sent from the "Let's get connected" form (also emailed to you).

The homepage is the page using the "Emma Divines Home" template.
